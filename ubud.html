FAQ

Common questions

Can I get my iPhone repaired in Ubud without going to Denpasar?
Yes. Our workshop is on Jl. Suweta in Sambahan, a few minutes from Ubud centre. Screen and battery replacements are done on site, usually within an hour.
How long does a battery replacement take?
About 45 minutes for an iPhone. Message us on WhatsApp beforehand with your model and we will have the battery ready when you arrive.
My MacBook got wet in the rain. Can you help?
Yes — liquid damage is one of our most common repairs during the rainy season. Keep the device switched off and bring it in as soon as possible so we can clean the board before corrosion spreads.
Do you charge for diagnostics?
No. Diagnostics are free and we confirm the exact repair price with you before we start. If you decide not to proceed, you pay nothing.
Which Apple devices do you repair?
All of them — iPhone, MacBook, iPad, Apple Watch, AirPods and iMac. iPhone and MacBook are the devices we see most often.
Where exactly is FixBox Ubud?
